# Nanite Swarm Concept Art

This set establishes the visual direction for the campaign: an indifferent,
industrial machine intelligence converting hostile worlds through precise,
physical logistics.

## Plates

- [Seedship Landing](01_seedship_landing.png) - the first deployment and the
  smallest readable expression of the Core.
- [Mars Dust District](02_mars_dust_district.png) - a mature surface network
  under dust pressure, with drills, processors, sweepers, and routed freight.
- [Venus Bridge Network](03_venus_bridge_network.png) - ceramic bridges,
  shield generators, and geothermal infrastructure spanning volcanic voids.
- [Core Evolution Sheet](04_core_evolution_sheet.png) - the Core's four-stage
  progression from crash pod to orbital ring.
- [Worker Drone Study](05_worker_drone_study.png) - carrier, sweeper, repair,
  and small nanite-unit shape language.

## Shared Direction

- Cyan-blue energy and mineral light against oxidized red, black basalt, and
  gunmetal.
- Modular hard-surface machinery with visible conduits and practical wear.
- The network is built tile by tile and remains legible as a graph.
- The swarm is purposeful and non-human: no faces, mascots, or weapon fantasy.
- Natural terrain should transition visibly into ordered synthetic geometry.

These are concept references, not runtime assets. They are intentionally kept
outside the asset registry until a final in-game illustration or replacement
asset is selected.
